# AIAP Patent Policy

AIAP is intended to remain implementable on royalty-free terms.

## Disclosure
A contributor proposing a mandatory Core requirement SHOULD disclose any patent or published patent application known to the contributor that the contributor reasonably believes would be necessarily infringed by a conforming implementation of that proposal.

## Project-owned essential claims
To the extent the Project Maintainer owns patent claims that become necessarily infringed solely by implementing an official mandatory AIAP Core requirement, the project's stated policy is to make such claims available for conforming AIAP implementations on royalty-free terms, subject to defensive termination for patent aggression.

## Limits
This policy does not grant rights in third-party patents, does not constitute a patent clearance opinion, and is not a substitute for legal advice. Before relying on this policy for a standards organization, certification program, or major commercial deployment, formal legal review is recommended.
