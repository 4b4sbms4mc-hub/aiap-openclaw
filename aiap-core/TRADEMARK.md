# AIAP Trademark Policy

The names **AI Avatar Protocol** and **AIAP**, official project logos, and any future **AIAP Certified** marks identify the official AIAP project.

## Permitted descriptive use
Accurate statements such as the following are permitted:
- “implements AIAP v0.3”
- “compatible with the AI Avatar Protocol”
- “an independent implementation of AIAP”

Such use must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, certification, or official project status.

## Modified versions
A modified or forked specification must not be presented as an official AIAP release unless authorized by the project.

## Product names and domains
Third parties should not use AIAP project marks in a way likely to cause confusion about source, ownership, or official status.

## Certification marks
No implementation may claim **AIAP Certified** status unless and until a formal certification program exists and the implementation satisfies its published requirements.

## Open-standard implementation rights
Nothing in this trademark policy restricts the right to implement the AIAP technical specification under the applicable licenses.
